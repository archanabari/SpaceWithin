Is Your Teen Struggling With Something They Can’t Explain?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

My teen is very quiet and defensive. What if they do not cooperate?

This happens very often. Many teens are not against growth. They are against pressure. We do not force teens to speak or open up immediately. We create emotional safety first. Trust builds slowly. Participation follows naturally.

Do parents sit in on these sessions?

No, the sessions are exclusively for the teens. For a teenager to truly open up and reflect, they need a space where they feel independent and completely unjudged. However, we do keep parents informed about the overall progress and general shifts we are seeing, without breaking the teen's trust or sharing their private reflections.

Is this program conducted online or in-person?

We offer both formats. We have an Online Batch conducted live via Google Meet, as well as an Offline Batch held in-person in Pune. Both formats are kept as small groups to ensure high interaction and personal attention.

Will a 7-session program really make a difference?

No genuine change happens overnight. But when teens receive practical, emotionally safe, and consistent guidance, meaningful inner shifts can begin. This program focuses on building a stronger inner foundation that continues helping them even after the sessions end.

My child already knows what to do. They just don’t do it.

That is exactly where many teens struggle. The problem is often not lack of intelligence or knowledge. The real struggle is fear of failure, overthinking, emotional overload, low self-belief, or inner confusion. We do not just tell teens what to do. We help them understand what is stopping them internally.
